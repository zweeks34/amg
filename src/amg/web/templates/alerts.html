Calendar

The next 12 months of jobs for every car, as all-day events on the days they fall due.

Download the calendar (.ics)

In Google Calendar: Settings → Import & export → Import, into a calendar of its own (say “Car maintenance”). The weekly email brings a fresh file each Monday. If importing a newer one adds copies instead of moving the dates, delete that calendar and import the new file into a fresh one.
